Frequently asked questions

A few things advisers usually ask.

Is the trial fully functional?

Yes. The trial is designed to let you explore Adviser CRM's standard functionality and understand how it could fit your business.

Can other people in my business join the trial?

Yes. You can include up to two additional users so your colleagues can explore Adviser CRM with you.

What happens after 14 days?

There is no obligation to continue. We will talk with you about your experience and, when Adviser CRM is right for you, help you choose the appropriate subscriptions.

Can you help us move from another CRM?

Yes. Adviser CRM provides data migration services for businesses moving from other systems. We can discuss the available options and the quality of your source data.

Is training available?

Yes. Personal onboarding and training are available to help your team get comfortable with the platform.
