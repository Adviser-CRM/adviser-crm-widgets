Introduction

Adviser CRM (“Adviser CRM”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy and handling personal information in accordance with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 and applicable privacy laws.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our website, products, and services.

“Personal information” means information about an identifiable individual.

By using our services or providing personal information to us, you agree to the collection, use, disclosure, and storage of your information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal obligations, or privacy practices.

Any updated version will be published on our website and will take effect from the date it is published.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

What Personal Information We Collect

We may collect personal information including:

  • your name, address, email address, and phone number
  • business and employment information
  • billing and payment information
  • account login credentials
  • communications with us
  • usage information relating to our website, software, and services
  • technical information such as IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, and cookies
  • any other information you voluntarily provide to us.

How We Collect Personal Information

We collect personal information from:

  • you directly, including when you contact us, register for or use our services, complete forms, subscribe to communications, interact with our website, or request support or information
  • third parties where you have authorised the collection, the information is publicly available, or the disclosure is otherwise permitted by law.

Where practicable, we will collect personal information directly from you.

Why We Collect and Use Personal Information

We may collect and use your personal information to:

  • verify your identity
  • provide and manage our products and services
  • communicate with you
  • provide customer support
  • improve our products, services, systems, and user experience
  • process transactions and payments
  • undertake credit or fraud checks where necessary
  • send service-related notices and updates
  • send marketing communications where permitted by law
  • conduct research, analytics, and statistical reporting
  • maintain security and prevent misuse of our systems
  • comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • enforce our legal rights
  • for any other purpose authorised by you or permitted by law.

Marketing Communications

We may send you marketing and promotional communications electronically, including by email or SMS, where permitted by applicable law.

You may opt out of receiving marketing communications at any time by:

  • clicking the unsubscribe link in electronic communications, or
  • contacting us directly.

Opting out of marketing communications will not affect essential service or account-related communications.

Disclosure of Personal Information

We may disclose personal information to:

  • related companies and affiliated entities
  • service providers and technology partners who assist us in operating our business and services
  • payment processors and financial institutions
  • professional advisers including lawyers, accountants, and auditors
  • regulatory authorities, government agencies, or law enforcement bodies where required or authorised by law
  • purchasers or potential purchasers of our business or assets
  • any other person authorised by you or permitted by law.

Some third-party service providers may store or process personal information outside New Zealand.

Where personal information is disclosed overseas, we will take reasonable steps to ensure the recipient is subject to privacy safeguards that provide comparable protection to New Zealand privacy laws, as required under the Privacy Act 2020.

Storage and Security

We take reasonable safeguards to protect personal information against:

  • loss
  • unauthorised access
  • misuse
  • disclosure
  • alteration
  • destruction.

These safeguards include technical, administrative, and physical security measures appropriate to the nature of the information we hold.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to:

  • fulfil the purposes for which it was collected
  • comply with legal obligations
  • resolve disputes
  • enforce agreements and legal rights.

When personal information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to securely destroy or de-identify it.

Privacy Breaches

If we become aware of a privacy breach that has caused, or is likely to cause, serious harm, we will comply with our obligations under the Privacy Act 2020, including notifying affected individuals and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner where required.

Accessing and Correcting Your Information

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you and request correction of that information.

Before providing access or making corrections, we may require proof of identity.

If we refuse a correction request, we will provide reasons where required and, if appropriate, note your requested correction alongside the information.

Requests can be made through our Contact Us page.

We may charge reasonable costs permitted by law for providing copies of personal information.

Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • operate website functionality
  • improve website performance and user experience
  • analyse website traffic and usage
  • personalise content and services.

You can disable cookies through your browser settings, although this may affect website functionality.

We may also use third-party analytics services that collect anonymised usage information.

Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies.

International Users

If you access our services from outside New Zealand, you acknowledge that your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in New Zealand or other countries where our service providers operate.
